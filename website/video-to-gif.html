Video to GIF

Make an animated GIF from a short clip – no watermark.

🔒 Free · no upload · no sign-up · no watermark

Convert audio or video

For example .wav → .mp3, .mov → .mp4, a video's sound → .mp3, or a short clip → .gif.

Drop audio or video hereor click to choose files

    The converter (about 32 MB) loads the first time you use it. Video takes longer in a browser than in the desktop Converter, which is also better for files over 1 GB.

    How to video to GIF

    1. Drop a short video in the box.
    2. Keep GIF selected.
    3. Click Convert and save.

    Private by design

    Your files never leave your device: everything happens inside your browser, nothing is uploaded or stored, and there's no sign-up, watermark or limit. Close the page and it's all gone.

    Questions

    How long can the GIF be?

    The first 20 seconds are used, at 12 frames per second and up to 480 px wide, to keep the file small.

    Is there a watermark?

    Never.
