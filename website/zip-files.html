    How to zip files

    1. Drop your files in the box.
    2. Give the zip a name.
    3. Click Make zip and save it.

    Private by design

    Your files never leave your device: everything happens inside your browser, nothing is uploaded or stored, and there's no sign-up, watermark or limit. Close the page and it's all gone.

    Questions

    Can I zip folders?

    Drop the files from inside the folder. The free Ethical Converter desktop app zips whole folders (and 7z).

    Are my files uploaded?

    No – the zip is made in your browser.
