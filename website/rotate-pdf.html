    How to rotate PDF pages

    1. Drop a PDF in the box.
    2. Choose how far to turn it, and which pages (leave empty for all).
    3. Click Rotate and save.

    Private by design

    Your files never leave your device: everything happens inside your browser, nothing is uploaded or stored, and there's no sign-up, watermark or limit. Close the page and it's all gone.

    Questions

    Can I rotate only some pages?

    Yes – type them in the Pages box, like 2, 4-6.

    Does rotating lower the quality?

    No. The pages are turned, not re-drawn, so text and pictures stay exactly as sharp.
