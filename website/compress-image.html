Compress images

Shrink photos so they're quick to send and upload – many at once.

🔒 Free · no upload · no sign-up · no watermark

Convert, resize & compress pictures

Change the format, make pictures smaller for email or the web, or shrink the file size. Also removes hidden photo data such as GPS location.

Drop pictures hereJPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF

    How to compress images

    1. Drop your pictures in the box.
    2. Pick the quality and (optionally) a smaller size.
    3. Click Convert – you'll see how much smaller each one got.

    Private by design

    Your files never leave your device: everything happens inside your browser, nothing is uploaded or stored, and there's no sign-up, watermark or limit. Close the page and it's all gone.

    Questions

    How much smaller do photos get?

    Phone photos usually shrink by 70–90 % with “Small file” and 1920 px.

    Does it remove location data?

    Yes. The new pictures don't carry the hidden photo data (like GPS location) of the originals.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser on your own device.
