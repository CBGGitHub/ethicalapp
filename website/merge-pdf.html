Merge PDFs

Put several PDFs together into one. Use the arrows to change the order.

Drop PDFs hereor click to choose files

    How to merge PDF files

    1. Drop your PDF files in the box (or click to choose them).
    2. Use the arrows to put them in the right order.
    3. Click Merge and save the new PDF.

    Private by design

    Your files never leave your device: everything happens inside your browser, nothing is uploaded or stored, and there's no sign-up, watermark or limit. Close the page and it's all gone.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No limits, no watermark and no account – merge as many PDFs as you like.

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. The PDFs are joined by your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not in the browser. The free Ethical PDF Editor desktop app opens protected PDFs.

    Is there a size limit?

    Only your device's memory. Hundreds of pages are no problem on a normal computer.
