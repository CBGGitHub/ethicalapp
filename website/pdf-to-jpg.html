    How to pDF to JPG

    1. Drop a PDF in the box.
    2. Choose JPG or PNG and the quality (dpi).
    3. Click Convert and save the pictures (or all as a .zip).

    Private by design

    Your files never leave your device: everything happens inside your browser, nothing is uploaded or stored, and there's no sign-up, watermark or limit. Close the page and it's all gone.

    Questions

    JPG or PNG?

    JPG files are smaller; PNG is sharper for text and drawings.

    What dpi should I use?

    150 dpi is good for screens and email, 300 dpi for printing.
