Split or extract pages

Take some pages out into a new PDF, or split every page into its own file.

Drop a PDF hereor click to choose a file

    How to split a PDF or extract pages

    1. Drop a PDF in the box.
    2. Choose what to do and type the pages, like 1-3, 5, 8-.
    3. Click Split and save the new PDFs (or all of them as one .zip).

    Private by design

    Your files never leave your device: everything happens inside your browser, nothing is uploaded or stored, and there's no sign-up, watermark or limit. Close the page and it's all gone.

    Questions

    How do I write the pages?

    Use commas and dashes: 1-3, 5, 8- means pages 1 to 3, page 5, and page 8 to the end.

    Is the original PDF changed?

    No. You always get new files; your original stays as it is.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. The PDF is split in your browser on your own device.
